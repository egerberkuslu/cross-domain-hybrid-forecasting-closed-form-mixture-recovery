## 0. Why round 5, and a disclosure

Round 5 is the fifth repair attempt of the Paper A claim (rounds 1 to 4 preceded it), and the paper reports it as such whatever the outcome.

Round 4 (300 cells of 60 series) met four of its five accuracy conditions and failed the fourth by 0.002 percentage points: 66 cells won after Holm at the primary minimum effect (50 at 0.5 %) against 24 required, none was significantly worse, the pooled gain over Chronos-Bolt was +0.604 % (cell interval 0.52 to 0.69 %, series-clustered 0.46 to 0.77 %), the median cell gain was +0.35 %, but the gain over the BETTER OF TWO random controls was +0.14 % with a series-clustered interval of -0.002 to +0.288 %. The monitor rule failed with 9, 11 and 8 of 12 traces against 10 required (`round4/r4_test_summary.json`, `r4_monitor_summary.json`).

Disclosure. The changed condition 4 of section 4 is motivated by the round 4 TEST results. Applied to the round 4 cells the new statistic would have passed (`round5/r4_data_check.txt`: each control alone, series-clustered one-sided p 1e-4, Holm-adjusted 2e-4), so the change makes the condition easier to pass than round 4 and the registration says so. The reasons for it are measured and stated before any pool-5 result: the per-cell minimum of two noisy controls is optimistic by construction (round 4: permuted control -0.05 %, null expert +0.17 %, per-cell better control +0.46 % against Chronos-Bolt), and the two controls answer two separate questions (permuted rows: does the alignment of E-Q with the target matter; null expert: does a model trained on shuffled features give the same gain through shrinkage alone). Each is tested separately and both must clear, with Holm over the two, and the pooled gain over each control must also be at least the minimum effect m of the rule (0.219 % primary, 0.5 % secondary), so that a significant but negligible gain over a control cannot pass; on the round 4 cells the pooled gains over the two controls are +0.635 % and +0.429 %, which clears the primary minimum and misses the secondary one for the null expert (`round5/r4_data_check.txt`). The best-of-two comparison of round 4 is still computed and reported (not a condition). The recipe, the weight guard, the minimum effects and the other four conditions are unchanged.

## 4. The accuracy rule (stratum A, 520 cells)

Comparison, controls and per-cell statistics are those of round 4 (`round4/REPAIR_PREREG_4.md` section 4): per cell the guarded mixture Bolt + alpha_final (E-Q - Bolt) against Chronos-Bolt, seed-free paired two-sided sign-flip block permutation (block h), Holm over all 520 cells, relative RMSE change with a moving-block bootstrap interval, TOST and identical cells reported; control a = E-Q rows permuted in blocks of h with the same weight (mean of 50 permutations), control b = the null expert E-Q0 with its own guarded weight. Two computational changes, both fixed before any pool-5 value exists: the permutation has B = 400,000 draws (round 4: 20,000), chosen for Monte Carlo precision near the Holm threshold and not as a cap: with 520 hypotheses the step-down threshold of the first ranks is about 1e-4, so at B = 20,000 the Holm decision of a cell near the threshold would rest on a handful of permutation draws and the smallest attainable raw p (5e-5) would sit at the threshold itself; the seed of the control permutations is 20261015.

Success (all required; m = 0.219 % = 0.5 % x 0.4375 is the primary minimum effect, retention of the guard G2(0.25), confirmed in round 4; m = 0.5 % in the secondary version):

1. at least ceil(0.08 x 520) = 42 cells better than Chronos-Bolt after Holm with a relative RMSE gain of at least m;
2. no cell significantly worse after Holm;
3. the pooled gain over Chronos-Bolt (mean over cells) is at least m and its lower 95 % bound is above 0 under the cell-level bootstrap and under the series-clustered two-stage bootstrap (series resampled, then cells within a series, B = 10,000);
4. CHANGED. For each control c in {a, b} the pooled gain 1 - RMSE(mixture)/RMSE(c) has a series-clustered two-stage bootstrap (B = 10,000) one-sided p = (1 + #(replicates <= 0)) / (B + 1); the two p values are Holm-adjusted and both adjusted values must be below 0.05 (equivalent to the series-clustered interval of each control lying above 0 with the Holm correction for two controls), and the pooled gain over each control must be at least m (0.219 % in the primary version, 0.5 % in the secondary version). The implementation of this clause in `r5_eval.summarize` is, inside `rule(m)`, `ctrl_ok and ca["mean"] >= m and cb["mean"] >= m`. The cell-level interval and the round 4 best-of-two comparison are reported and not conditions;
5. the median cell gain over Chronos-Bolt is above 0.

Reporting that is part of the registration: per source (CESNET, GEANT) cells, wins, mean and median gain, gain over each control; the number of series with a negative gain over each control; the best-of-two comparison; cells better, worse and identical. The statistics are implemented in `round5/r5_eval.py` (sha256 `7b3bfb7797097563399fe3bf9bef071d45c4156931fe807652b6e62c33b6cd0c`); its series bootstrap reproduces `stats_tools.cell_cluster_boot_mean` on the round 4 cells (interval ends within 5e-5, `r4_data_check.txt`).

## 5. The monitor (stratum F, 64 drifting traces)

Detectors, calibration, grids, validation budget, burn-in (100 decisions counted in every false-alarm denominator), injection change points {420, 420 + 0.5 (n - 110 - 420), n - 110}, ten injection seeds, the matched no-injection control and the placebo clocks P_j = 190 + 30 j (lag cap 100) are those of round 4 (`round4/r4_monitor.py`, imported unchanged). Traces: the 64 stratum-F series of section 2 (11 CESNET, 53 Abilene), h = 6, unguarded forecasts of E-Q and Chronos-Bolt, one cell per trace.

Success for RF (all required, n = 64, need = ceil(0.8 n) = 52, the round 4 fraction): condition 1, no-fault false-alarm rate at most 5 % on at least 52 traces; condition 2, shift-specific detection (Newcombe interval against the matched no-injection control above 0) on at least 52 traces; condition 3, detection above the placebo rate (Newcombe interval above 0) and shift-specific on at least min(52, number of traces satisfying condition 2) traces. The three conditions above are kept as the registered rule, but the rule is declared uninformative in advance (power 0.02 at 64 traces, below); the monitor result is therefore a descriptive estimate, and the registration states what is reported: pass rates per condition with Wilson 95 % intervals, overall and per source (CESNET, Abilene). The Abilene traces are OD components of one network over the same 699 test windows and are not independent, so their Wilson intervals are optimistic and the per-source Abilene rates are read as one network observed 53 times, not as 53 independent replications. No monitor claim enters the abstract, contributions, highlights or cover letter, whatever the rates are, and no new lower pass-rate threshold is introduced after the round 4 result; the paper reports the rates, the intervals and the (failed or passed) 80 % rule together with the power statement.

Power (`round5/power_monitor5.py` sha256 `f1fa6360f71bc1b3c8c695e75a39d90ee7e3d4fc4cf37683589269c475afbd2a`, `power_monitor5.json` sha256 `ad1237269f142fb697f0df375d76bc665a2c179117a4e14864386742e6c8a10d`). Round 4 per-trace pass patterns (RF; condition 1 on 9 of 12, condition 2 on 11, condition 3 on 8; CESNET 4/5/4 of 6, Abilene 5/6/4 of 6), a Dirichlet(0.5) posterior over the eight patterns per source, independent traces. Power of the rule: 0.03 for the round 4 design of 12 traces, 0.02 for 30 and 0.02 for all 64 traces. If every condition passed with a true probability of 0.90 per trace the power would be 0.70, 0.94 and 0.97, and at 0.95 it would be 0.95, 1.00 and 1.00. The trace count therefore does reach power 0.8 at 64 traces, but only if the true per-trace pass probability is about 0.9 or higher; at the pass rates measured in round 4 (0.75, 0.92 and 0.67) the rule is expected to fail at any trace count, because it asks for 80 % of traces to pass conditions whose estimated pass rates are 67 % to 75 %. The monitor is frozen (no change of detector, grid or calibration is made after the round 4 result) so this round measures the pass rates on 64 new traces with a confidence interval, and the paper will report the miss, if it comes, together with this power statement and the descriptive rates; the 80 % rule is recorded here and is not the basis of any claim.
